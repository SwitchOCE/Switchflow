# Linked discovery questions

Use when a question needs its own research, prototype, dependencies or session, or one checkpoint no longer makes the open questions clear. Continue the same intake.

## Expand visible questions

Orient to the destination and exclusions. Create a child for each precise question needing separate work; label it `discovery` and one of `research`, `prototype`, `discussion`, or `prerequisite`. Keep it outside delivery milestones and phase labels. Create records first, then wire native dependencies with their actual IDs.

The parent is an index: link resolved answers by name and a one-line consequence. Open questions point to children instead of repeating bodies. Keep uncertainty that cannot yet be phrased in Not yet specified; remove it there when it becomes a child. Excluded work returns only when the owner changes scope.

Each child states its question, coverage and stop condition, answer owner and required evidence. Research may inspect named source areas or primary external sources, returning facts, citations, limitations and the decision they inform. Keep source exploration in that bounded investigation's context rather than every intake instance.

Prototypes answer one question through a cheap local artifact and observation. Keep them separate from production code; link location and findings. Present behaviour or alternatives for owner judgement; creating a prototype does not authorize choosing an owner-owned answer. Prerequisite tasks exist only to enable a decision, such as arranging access; live actions retain their approval boundaries.

## Resume and resolve

Read the index and child summaries. Choose an unblocked question relevant to the next decision; fetch related answers only as needed. Check the recorded writer before claiming work. Single-writer discovery is the default. Separately authorized research can run independently when questions and outputs do not overlap; record assignments to prevent duplicate work.

Resolve discussion with the actual owner, never an agent-simulated answer. Record resolution, rationale, evidence and its source on the question, then apply doc-03's discovery acceptance rule. Update the parent's index and next step. New precise questions become children; invalidated questions receive superseding comments and revised dependencies. Archive obsolete questions with their reason instead of deleting history or calling unanswered questions resolved.

Surface evidence contradicting a settled decision with its downstream effects. Return it to the appropriate owner; an already-frozen milestone stays unchanged until `edit-milestone` applies the accepted revision. Refresh affected questions rather than designing around stale answers.

Checkpoint at a coherent decision boundary; continue related questions while context is useful rather than requiring a new session per question. A fresh instance should need only the parent, next question and decisive evidence. Finish by synthesizing the contract through `intake`. Discovery produces no execution grant, including through agent-written notes.
